CONFIDENTIAL – RFP Exhibit B - AMAZON eCOMMERCE & 3PL SAP SYSTEMS INTEGRATION RFP Scope and Business Requirements American Honda Motor Co., Inc. (AHM) seeks a qualified IT systems integration supplier to design, configure, develop, test, deploy, and stabilize the SAP and middleware interfaces needed to support Honda Power Equipment eCommerce fulfillment through Amazon Marketplace and a separately sourced third-party logistics (3PL) provider. The engagement covers HAL SAP ECC, Global SAP ECC (GSAP), Amazon Marketplace, the selected third-party logistics (3PL) Warehouse Management System (WMS), and SAP Cloud Platform Integration (SAP CPI), which is required for applicable Honda-side integration work. The 3PL solution is being procured separately. Honda anticipates providing available additional technical information through the RFP clarification process. Objectives: • Enable automated order-to-fulfillment processing and timely delivery, status, and tracking integration. • Provide accurate inventory, returns/replacements, serial/VIN, and exception visibility between Honda and its 3PL. • Integration method. Use SAP CPI for applicable Honda-side ERP integration work. Specific topology, interfaces, connectivity, and platform provisioning remain subject to Honda technical validation. • Business capability. Support GSAP–Amazon financial data exchange, Amazon Fulfilled by Merchant (FBM) operations, and assessment of potential Seller Fulfilled Prime (SFP) readiness should Honda elect to adopt SFP. • Deliver controlled design, integration testing, cutover, knowledge transfer, and 60 calendar days of hypercare. Project reference: PR11876 – Amazon System Readiness Complete the Supplier Questionnaire, Pricing Worksheet and Compliance worksheets in Exhibit C – Honda eCommerce & 3PL SAP Systems Integration RFP Questionnaire and Pricing Sheet. Provide supporting technical detail, proposed service levels, assumptions, exceptions, and any pricing detail that does not fit the existing worksheets as clearly labeled proposal attachments. Engagement Overview and Scope Boundaries The detailed design of the 3PL WMS and its connections to Honda systems has not been finalized. Requirements in this Exhibit describe the expected business capabilities and the supplier services to be proposed. Architecture, interface specifications, technical ownership and program-dependent requirements will be confirmed through the formal design and RFP clarification processes. Suppliers shall identify material assumptions or dependencies in their proposals and, where applicable, in the Compliance worksheet. 1.1 Purpose and expected outcomes The Supplier shall design, develop, configure, test, deploy, and support integrations for Amazon-originated orders across the Honda systems and selected 3PL WMS, in accordance with the final approved interface inventory. The documented outcome areas are order processing, inventory synchronization, shipment and tracking information, returns/replacements, product traceability, and GSAP–Amazon financial data exchange. Future channels, additional fulfillment nodes, or SAP platform transitions are not part of a committed day-one scope without further Honda authorization. • Initial business model. Design for Amazon Fulfilled by Merchant (FBM) and assess the integration capabilities needed to support Seller Fulfilled Prime (SFP) if Honda elects to adopt it. SFP activation, program eligibility, and specific operating rules are not prescribed in this RFP. • Business outcomes. Enable order automation, near-real-time inventory visibility, timely shipment and tracking data, auditable financial integration, exception management, product traceability, recall/hazmat controls, and measurable operational performance. 1.2 In-scope platforms and integration ecosystem • Honda ERP landscape. HAL SAP ECC supports relevant order, outbound delivery, inventory and related transactions; GSAP supports applicable financial transactions. The selected 3PL solution is expected to support interoperability with Honda ERP technologies, including SAP ECC and potential SAP S/4HANA environments. An SAP migration is not included in this RFP unless separately authorized. • Mandatory integration method. SAP Cloud Platform Integration (SAP CPI) is required for the applicable Honda-side integration work covered by this RFP. The exact interface routes, CPI tenant and license arrangements, transport protocols, and connections to Amazon or the selected 3PL are not yet defined. Suppliers shall document proposed patterns for Honda IT approval and shall not assume an approved direct, indirect, or bypass connection. • Potential external interfaces. The integration ecosystem includes Amazon Marketplace, the selected 3PL WMS, and relevant carrier/transportation interfaces. The final placement of CPI and other authorized components in each connection will be defined through Honda-approved solution design. • Environments. The design shall cover authorized development, system integration test, user acceptance test, and production environments, including controlled promotion and environment-specific security and configuration. 1.3 3PL procurement dependency and boundary management Honda is procuring the 3PL solution through a separate sourcing process. Detailed WMS configuration, hosting, supported APIs and message formats, endpoint and authentication details, transaction timing, and technical ownership will be provided when available. Suppliers should price the defined scope and explicitly identify dependencies on details to be confirmed. • Responsibility boundaries. Warehouse operations, fulfillment, physical labeling, inventory handling and physical returns are within the separately procured 3PL service scope. The selected IT integration supplier will be responsible for the integration services and deliverables assigned under the final agreement. Cross-provider interface ownership and operational handoffs will be established during solution design. • Approval and ownership. Honda will approve applicable requirements, architecture, testing, deployment and acceptance deliverables in writing. System-of-record definitions, technical ownership and ongoing support boundaries will be confirmed before implementation. • Future clarifications. Honda anticipates distributing additional available 3PL technical and integration information through an RFP addendum. Suppliers shall distinguish their proposed solution from dependencies requiring confirmation and describe any scope, cost or schedule implications in their proposals. Complete the Supplier Questionnaire, Pricing Worksheet and Compliance worksheets in Exhibit C. The IT Flows Reference and CPI & 3PL Requirements worksheets are provided for reference only. Detailed cross-provider responsibility allocation and selected 3PL configuration will be confirmed during subsequent design.
